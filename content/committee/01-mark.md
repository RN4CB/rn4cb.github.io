A/Prof Mark Ziemann is a computational biology researcher specializing in genomics, transcriptomics, and reproducible high-throughput data analysis. His research focuses on improving the rigor, reliability, and interpretability of RNA-seq and multi-omics analyses through methodological evaluation, statistical benchmarking, and transparent workflow development. He is the lead developer of two R/Bioconductor packages and the lead maintainer of the DEE2 database of processed transcriptome data. Mark is an internationally recognised expert on statistically rigourous pathway enrichment analysis. In 2024 he was awarded with the MAQC Society Outstanding Reproducibility in Science Award for his work entitled *"The five pillars of computational reproducibility: bioinformatics and beyond"*. He is currently employed at Burnet Institute, Australia, where his working group is building capacity and capability to support the analytical needs of the institute.

Mark co-founded RN4CB because he's passionate about research rigour, public trust in science and seeing advances in biomedicine translated into the clinic.

Featured article: Wijesooriya K, Jadaan SA, Perera KL, Kaur T, Ziemann M. Urgent need for consistent standards in functional enrichment analysis. *PLoS Comput Biol*. 2022 Mar 9;18(3):e1009935. [doi: 10.1371/journal.pcbi.1009935](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pcbi.1009935).
