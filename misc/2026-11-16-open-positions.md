title: Open Positions on the Steering Committee
date: 16th November 2026
Author: Mark Ziemann
---

RN4CB runs on the collective input from our volunteers, especially the steering committee.
Currently we have two open positions in the committee which will be central to the success
of the network.

Firstly, we are seeking a person who wil lead our career development and awards initiatives.
This role will be to develop a strategy and roadmap for career development of early-career
researchers in our network.
A central part of the role will be to coordinate annual prizes to promote achievements
in reproducibility in computational biology.

Secondly, we seek someone who will focus on identifying and developing funding initiatives.
This second role will involve applying for funding from agencies and potential commercial sponsors.
These funds will be used to give small grants for research projects, travel grants and excellence awards.

These roles require about 1 hour of work per week and you will receive support from me and others on the committee.
These are suited to people with active research roles from PhD candidates up to professor level.
If this sounds like you, I'd be very happy to hear from you by email.

We especially welcome nominations from women and people from underrepresented countries.
